## Executive Summary

The PEI-phase counterpart of `XhcInitializeDeviceSlot`. Three allocations lack proper NULL checks: (1) `InputContext` at line 1122 with ASSERT-only, then `ZeroMem` at 1125; (2) `EndpointTransferRing` at line 1183 with NO NULL CHECK AT ALL, passed to `XhcPeiCreateTransferRing` at 1185; (3) `OutputContext` at line 1225 with ASSERT-only, then `ZeroMem` at 1228.

This advisory is one of a set reported to VulnCheck on 2026-06-25 for coordinated disclosure to the EDK II maintainers. After several follow-ups over the following months no response was received. 126 days after the initial report, ByteRay is publishing the findings in good faith so that affected integrators can assess and remediate them. All findings were identified against EDK II commit `70741a15d9a714323a5598eac47727e60507e22f` (master, 2026-06-21).

## Affected Products and Versions

- Vendor: TianoCore
- Product: EDK II (`MdeModulePkg`)
- Affected commit: `70741a15d9a714323a5598eac47727e60507e22f` (master, 2026-06-21). The code is present on that commit and later revisions that have not changed it.
- **Location:** `MdeModulePkg/Bus/Pci/XhciPei/XhciSched.c:1122`
- **Function:** `XhcPeiInitializeDeviceSlot`
- **Category:** `error_handling`

## Technical Details

**Controllability:** `SEMI_TRUSTED` — confidence: **high**. Physical USB device triggers PEI-phase enumeration.

**Data Flow**

At line 1122, `InputContext = UsbHcAllocateMem(...)` with ASSERT-only at 1123, then `ZeroMem` at 1125. At line 1183, `EndpointTransferRing = AllocateZeroPool(...)` with NO CHECK AT ALL, passed to `XhcPeiCreateTransferRing` at 1185 which dereferences it. At line 1225, `OutputContext = UsbHcAllocateMem(...)` with ASSERT-only at 1226, then `ZeroMem` at 1228.

**Affected Callers**

- `XhcPeiPollPortStatusChange`

**Sanitization / Security Controls**

- DEBUG-only ASSERT (partial).

## Reachability and Preconditions

Attacker position and trust level for this issue are stated in the Controllability line and Data Flow above, and the concrete trigger is in Reproduction below.

**Exploitability:** **Low**  
**Realism:** **Low**

## Reproduction

1. Physical access to USB port; PEI-phase boot; RELEASE build; memory pressure.
2. Attacker connects physical USB device during PEI-phase boot.
3. `XhcPeiPollPortStatusChange` detects the USB connect and calls `XhcPeiInitializeDeviceSlot`.
4. At line 1122, `UsbHcAllocateMem` for `InputContext` returns NULL; `ASSERT` at 1123 is no-op; `ZeroMem` at 1125 dereferences NULL.
5. At line 1183, `AllocateZeroPool` for `EndpointTransferRing` returns NULL with NO CHECK; `XhcPeiCreateTransferRing` at 1185 dereferences NULL

## Impact

Denial of service — firmware crash during PEI USB enumeration.

## Remediation and Mitigation

As of commit `70741a15d9a714323a5598eac47727e60507e22f` this issue is unfixed upstream and no CVE has been assigned. Check the allocation result for NULL before dereferencing it and fail the operation cleanly when allocation fails. The specific control that is absent or ineffective is identified in the Sanitization / Security Controls list under Technical Details.
